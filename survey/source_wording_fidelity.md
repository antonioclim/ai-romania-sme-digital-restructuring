# Source-wording fidelity

The Romanian questionnaire preserves the wording recoverable from the paired exports. The English version is a documentary translation intended to make the instrument inspectable in an international research context. It was not independently administered.

The reconstruction does not silently correct methodological tensions in the source wording. In particular:

- employee-band classification does not establish the full legal definition of an SME
- the central AI-engagement item combines budgeting, testing and implementation in one project-stage category
- the workforce-preparation checklist permits a positive measure to coexist with “no specific measures”
- the return-on-investment item says that multiple answers may be selected although the export stores one category
- an economic-growth item uses “maximum 12% GDP” without specifying time horizon, baseline or denominator
- the environmental-impact item contains an inconsistent evaluative heading

These features are documented because retrospective correction would produce a cleaner instrument than the one evidenced by the exports. The reconstructed questionnaire should not be re-administered without independent review and validation.
