# Generative-AI assistance disclosure for the version 3 workflow

OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-5.6 Pro) assisted the development of the version 3 release candidate through language and structural editing, literature and reference cross-checking, drafting and review of reproducibility documentation, preparation and review of analytical and figure-generation code and construction of explanatory diagrams.

The author determined the research questions, conceptual distinctions, state definitions, analytical scope, evidential boundaries and interpretation. The author reviewed and, where necessary, revised all generated text, code, references, numerical outputs and graphical elements and assumes full responsibility for the work.

The underlying survey responses were not generated or altered using generative AI. Generative AI was not used to infer missing respondent data, adjudicate respondent identity, fabricate ethical approval or create unsupported empirical results.

For an Elsevier submission, the final manuscript will disclose:

- AI-assisted manuscript preparation in the dedicated declaration;
- AI-assisted analytical or figure-generation code in the Methods section;
- AI assistance in the caption of any explanatory figure whose initial construction used the tool;
- the human verification and reproducibility controls applied.
