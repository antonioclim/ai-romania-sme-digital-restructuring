# ODSA reporting checklist

A study reporting Outcome-Definition Sensitivity Analysis should disclose the following items.

## State and instrument register

- [ ] The observed states are listed exactly as collected or derived.
- [ ] Any many-to-one source category and irrecoverable coarsening are identified.
- [ ] The empirical unit and denominator are stated.
- [ ] Missingness, exclusions and possible duplicate units are reported.

## Definition register

- [ ] Every outcome definition has a stable name and positive-state set.
- [ ] The intended question for every definition is stated.
- [ ] Denominators are common or differences are explained.
- [ ] Definitions are identified as nested, disjoint or overlapping.

## Diagnostics

- [ ] Definition-specific levels and uncertainty are reported.
- [ ] Broader outcomes are decomposed by positive state.
- [ ] The same association statistic is used across compared definitions.
- [ ] Group-specific rates and any rank reversals are reported.
- [ ] Differences between dependent statistics are not presented as formal tests unless an appropriate procedure is used.

## Claims and interpretation

- [ ] Claim wording is registered separately from outcome coding.
- [ ] Positive states incompatible with a claim are listed.
- [ ] Adoption, implementation, routine use, capability and realised value are not treated as interchangeable.
- [ ] Population, causal and firm-level claims are limited to what the design supports.

## Reproducibility and governance

- [ ] The mapping from states to definitions is machine-readable.
- [ ] Tables and figures are generated from the registered inputs.
- [ ] Public and controlled data boundaries are explicit.
- [ ] Ethics approval, exemption or non-review status is described accurately.
- [ ] Informed-consent procedures are described.
- [ ] AI-assisted code, text or figure preparation is disclosed according to publisher policy.
