# Study 2 weighted-missingness interpretation register

## Permitted manuscript findings

- The complete-case analysis remains the primary Study 2 analysis.
- Weighted worst-case assignment of unresolved rows preserves the broad
  digitally enabled versus specialised separation in 15 of 16 eligible source
  strata.
- It preserves the broad digitally enabled versus ERP-only separation in 13
  of 14 eligible source strata.
- The specialised versus ERP-only separation is robust in 9 of 14 eligible
  source strata and is therefore not universal.
- The median unresolved-weight share is approximately 4.16%, although the
  maximum source-stratum share is approximately 19.08%.
- Pairwise source-stratum ordering is only partially determinate under the
  bounds.

## Mandatory wording

Use:

> weighted worst-case unresolved-row sensitivity bounds

Do not use:

> confidence intervals

> uncertainty intervals

> posterior intervals

> design-based intervals

## Joint-definition rule

Marginal lower and upper values must not be combined as if they were jointly
attainable. Cross-definition claims must be based on the coherent scenarios:
all handwritten, all broad-only, all specialised or all ERP.

## Prohibited claims

- a global or fifteen-country prevalence;
- causal effects;
- representative country rankings;
- a universal specialised-versus-ERP separation;
- proof that one definition is uniquely valid;
- sampling-uncertainty coverage;
- public release of the full internal IM-R7C JSON.

## Disclosure rule

Only cross-stratum summaries may be public. Source-stratum bound rows, exact
counts and exact weight sums remain private.
