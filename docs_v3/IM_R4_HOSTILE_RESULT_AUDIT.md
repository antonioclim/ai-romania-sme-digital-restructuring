# IM-R4 hostile audit of the frozen simulation results

## Verdict

The full simulation materially strengthens ODSA but does not make the article
submission-ready. It removes the defensible criticism that the framework is
supported only by three recodings of one survey item. It does not remove the
need for an independent Study 2, an institutionally defensible ethics
statement or exact article–code–table–figure parity.

## Claims that survive hostile review

### Level monotonicity is not the substantive result

No broader nested outcome fell below the narrower outcome in any of the
1,728,000 replicates. This is an arithmetic invariant under a common observed
denominator and must not be presented as a discovery.

### Association direction is not constrained by nesting

The frozen mechanisms produced positive, negative and zero population
broad-minus-active Cramér's V contrasts. A broader nested outcome can therefore
strengthen, weaken or remove an association without violating level
monotonicity.

### Subgroup order can change

The prespecified rank-reversal mechanism generated opposite population group
orders under the narrow and broad outcomes. Every replicate in the reference
n=250 cell contained at least one strict cross-definition reversal.

### Composition is indispensable

The project-stage share of the broad positive class varied by mechanism. A
broad headline rate therefore cannot be interpreted as active use unless all
states counted positive satisfy that wording.

### Observation error can change the apparent definition contrast

Ten per cent active/project swapping preserved the broad boundary under no
missingness but changed the active-use association. Ten per cent project/other
swapping crossed the broad boundary and changed the broad level and
association.

## Findings that require careful wording

- Cramér's V is non-negative. When a population association is zero, finite
  samples induce upward bias. This pulls observed contrasts towards zero in
  the project-only and compensating mechanisms.
- Sample strict reversals around a population tie are ranking instability, not
  population-level reversal.
- Monte Carlo frequencies are conditional on the six frozen mechanisms and
  must not be described as universal probabilities for ODSA.
- The simulation does not establish that an ODSA display improves managerial
  decisions.

## Remaining reviewer attacks

1. The main factorial programme uses three mutually exclusive states, three
   groups and a nested definition family.
2. The 10% misclassification mechanisms are nondifferential and deliberately
   simple.
3. Missingness is limited to state-independent and project-heavy profiles.
4. The claim-admissibility register still requires transparent substantive
   judgement by the researcher.
5. Generality beyond the Romanian application remains incomplete until Study
   2 is selected and analysed under the same prespecified core diagnostics.
6. The ethics determination remains external to the simulation and software.
