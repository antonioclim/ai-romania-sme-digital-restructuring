# Adjacent methods and the defensible novelty boundary

## 1. Locked novelty claim

ODSA must not be presented as the first sensitivity analysis, the first multiverse of operationalisations or a general solution to measurement validity. The defensible claim is narrower:

> Outcome-Definition Sensitivity Analysis is a formal inference-control audit for finite categorical outcome maps. It links an explicit observed-state register to definition relations, exact state-entry and state-exit accounting, positive-class composition, recoverability, association and subgroup-order diagnostics and a conservative check that every state counted positive remains compatible with the claim attached to the outcome.

The distinctive feature is the coupled audit. No individual ODSA statistic is claimed as novel.

## 2. Collision acknowledged

The general problem is not new.

- Multiverse analysis and specification curves expose sensitivity to defensible analytical choices.
- Multiverse operationalisations explicitly examine multiple ways of measuring a construct.
- Measurement-nonequivalence frameworks warn that defensible alternatives need not be interchangeable.
- Vibration-of-effects work separates instability due to modelling, measurement and sampling choices.
- Misclassification sensitivity quantifies consequences of erroneous observation categories.
- Information Systems research already shows that system-use conceptualisation and operationalisation change empirical conclusions.

ODSA therefore enters an occupied literature. Its contribution must be specified at the level of categorical state maps and inference control.

## 3. Comparison with adjacent approaches

| Approach | Primary object | Shared ground | Boundary ODSA must preserve |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multiverse analysis | Results across reasonable data-processing choices | Both expose sensitivity to defensible choices | A multiverse often assumes a stable focal question across branches. ODSA first checks whether the outcome definitions preserve that question. |
| Specification curve analysis | Results across theoretically justified, statistically valid and non-redundant model specifications | Both require an explicit specification set and complete reporting | ODSA does not pool definitions that represent different constructs or managerial questions and it does not claim joint inference across them. |
| Multiverse operationalisations | Associations across many construct operationalisations | Both study measurement choice | ODSA specialises in finite categorical state-to-outcome maps and requires state-entry, composition, recoverability and claim-admissibility audits. |
| Measurement-nonequivalence framework | Whether alternative choices are equivalent, nonequivalent or uncertain | Both reject automatic interchangeability | ODSA operationalises a narrow categorical case; it did not originate the general nonequivalence principle. |
| Many-analyst analysis | Variation produced by independent analytical teams | Both reveal that defensible choices can alter conclusions | ODSA is a prespecified audit of outcome construction, not a crowdsourced estimate of analyst heterogeneity. |
| Vibration of effects | Instability under model, measurement or sampling choices | Both separate sources of variation | ODSA's primary object is the outcome map and its semantic consequences rather than the distribution of fitted model coefficients. |
| Questionable measurement practices framework | Transparency about construct definition, operationalisation and scoring | Both treat measurement choices as consequential | ODSA cannot establish construct validity. It audits what a registered categorical definition visibly preserves or erases. |
| System-use conceptualisation | Context-specific conceptualisation and operationalisation of system use | Both require alignment between measure and theoretical question | ODSA does not replace substantive conceptualisation of users, systems, tasks or organisational context. |
| Item response theory and scale validation | Latent measurement, item properties and scale construction | Both challenge unexamined measurement conventions | ODSA is not a latent-variable model, scale-development method, reliability analysis or test of measurement invariance. |
| Misclassification sensitivity analysis | Bias from erroneous classification against an assumed truth | Both examine category mappings | ODSA's core problem can exist when every observed state is recorded correctly: deliberate definitions may still answer different questions. |

## 4. Why ODSA is not merely a small multiverse

A homogeneous multiverse or specification curve is most coherent when the estimand remains substantively stable. ODSA admits the possibility that two definitions are individually defensible yet nonequivalent because they represent different organisational states.

ODSA asks:

1. which observed states are positive under each definition;
2. how the definitions are related as sets;
3. which states enter and leave through a change in definition;
4. how each positive class is composed;
5. whether the same association and subgroup order survive;
6. whether the same claim remains admissible;
7. whether the source instrument has already destroyed a required distinction.

When the definitions answer different questions, the proper output is a structured comparison, not a pooled robustness verdict.

## 5. Information Systems positioning

Information Systems research already demonstrates that conceptualising and operationalising system use differently can change empirical conclusions. ODSA is a specialised extension for categorical outcome maps.

The journal-facing contribution is:

- outcome definition is part of IS construct operationalisation, not a neutral coding step;
- categorical broadening can change the organisational object represented by a KPI;
- positive-class composition should be reported rather than hidden behind one percentage;
- a claim must remain compatible with every state counted positive;
- irrecoverable coarsening must be disclosed rather than silently repaired;
- association strength and subgroup order need not be stable under nested broadening.

The Romanian AI application remains a worked example under severe design constraints and cannot validate a universal method by itself.

## 6. Defensible wording

- “a formal audit framework for categorical outcome definitions”
- “a specialised, state-based extension to existing sensitivity traditions”
- “an inference-control layer”
- “definition-sensitive level, composition, association and subgroup order”
- “claim admissibility under an explicit state register”
- “a worked application, frozen simulation programme and independent replication”
- “complementary to multiverse, specification-curve and misclassification approaches”

## 7. Indefensible wording

- “the first sensitivity analysis of measurement”
- “the first multiverse of operationalisations”
- “a new estimator”
- “a universal robustness test”
- “a validated scale”
- “a method that identifies the correct adoption rate”
- “proof that broad measures are invalid”
- “joint evidence across all definitions” when definitions answer different questions
- “proof that disclosure improves decisions” without a decision experiment
