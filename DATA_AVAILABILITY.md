# Data availability

This public software release contains aggregate counts, low-dimensional contingency tables, questionnaire documentation, generated tables, figure-source data and figures. It contains no respondent-level data.

The respondent-level survey material is not available for unrestricted public reuse. Removal of direct identifiers did not reduce the distinctiveness of the structured profiles and narrative answers sufficiently to support an anonymous-public-data claim. Any future controlled access requires an independent institutional decision, a defined and proportionate purpose, appropriate safeguards and a documented disclosure-risk assessment.

The absence of respondent rows does not prevent reproduction of the results reported in the accompanying analysis. Every public statistic is rebuilt from the aggregate inputs in `data/aggregate` and linked to a canonical output in `metadata/claim_evidence_ledger.csv`.
